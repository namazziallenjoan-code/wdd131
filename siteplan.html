🚗 Uganda Ride Hub

Website Planning Document

1. Site Name

Uganda Ride Hub

The name Uganda Ride Hub represents a website dedicated to providing car information and helping people in Uganda make informed decisions when choosing a vehicle. "Uganda" identifies the main target market, while "Ride Hub" represents a central place where people can discover, compare, and learn about cars.

Proposed domain: ugandaridehub.com

Logo idea: A simple car symbol combined with the Uganda Ride Hub name.

2. Site Purpose

Uganda Ride Hub will provide useful information about different cars available and commonly used in Uganda. The website will help visitors explore car brands and models, view vehicle pictures, learn about prices, fuel efficiency, features, advantages and disadvantages, and read ratings.

Visitors will also be able to compare vehicles, share their opinions, and learn from other people's experiences. The main goal is to make researching and choosing a car easier for people living in Uganda.

3. Scenarios

These are questions that potential website visitors may ask:

  • Which car is affordable, reliable, and fuel-efficient for driving in Uganda?
  • How much does a Toyota RAV4 or Toyota Premio cost in Uganda?
  • What are the advantages and disadvantages of owning a particular car model?
  • Which car has better fuel efficiency and performance when compared with another model?
  • Where can I read ratings and opinions from other car owners?

5. Typography

Two fonts will be used to maintain readability and a consistent appearance throughout the website.

Lora – Headings

Lora will be used for the main page title, section headings, and important titles. It gives the website a clean and professional appearance.

Roboto – Body Text

Roboto will be used for paragraphs, navigation links, buttons, descriptions, and other general website content because it is clear and easy to read.
